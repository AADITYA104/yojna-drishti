# Legal Notices and Branding Rules (working draft; not legal advice)

## Disclaimer content (page /about/disclaimer, bilingual, linked in every footer)
- Who operates the platform and in what capacity (owner organisation: OQ-08). If it is not an official government website, say so plainly: independent analytics platform using publicly available and authorised data.
- Figures are aggregates as published or supplied by the named sources; verification status and as-of dates are shown; users should check official portals for current scheme rules.
- DEMO data is synthetic and not government data.
- No personal data is collected about beneficiaries.
- Limitation of liability and grievance contact (counsel to review).

## Emblem and branding
Do not use the State Emblem of India, Gujarat government logos or department logos unless written authorisation is held and recorded (the Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950 restricts such use; confirm applicability with counsel). Use a text wordmark. Do not imply official endorsement.

## Source attribution
Each dataset shows its source name, URL, licence note and date. Boundary and basemap attributions mandatory.

## Terms of use (outline)
Acceptable use, no scraping beyond rate limits, citation request, data accuracy statement, change policy.

## Cookies and tracking
Only functional cookies; no analytics or ad trackers in v1 (privacy.md).

## Accessibility statement
Target WCAG 2.2 AA; known gaps listed after each audit; contact for feedback.

## Open items
Owner entity, jurisdiction, grievance officer, review by counsel.
